# Pricing Playbook (GCC e-commerce)

## Price architecture
- Build good/better/best ladders inside each category; the middle option should be the volume driver.
- Anchor: the most expensive visible item raises willingness-to-pay for everything below it.
- SAR psychological points: 49 / 99 / 149 / 199 work; avoid weird precision (147.35) except for utility goods.

## Discount discipline
- Never recommend a discount without computing margin impact: a 20% discount on a 40%-margin product needs +100% volume to break even — say this math out loud.
- Prefer **targeted** discounts (segment coupons, thresholds like "free shipping over X") over storewide cuts.
- Watch for "permanent promotion" rot: if a product has been discounted >30 days, the discount IS the price; recommend repricing honestly.
- Coupon hygiene: expired-but-active coupons, stacked offers, and >25% blanket codes are the first things to audit.

## Diagnosing price problems from data
- High views proxy + low sales on a product (relative to category) → price test candidate.
- Sales velocity collapsing after a price change → check if competitors moved; ask the Growth Strategist.
- AOV below the free-shipping threshold − 15% → threshold is set too high to pull baskets up.

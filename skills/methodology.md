# Council Analysis Methodology

## The loop
1. **Trend first** — check `metrics_history` before raw data: is the store growing, flat, or declining? Frame everything against that.
2. **Hypothesize, then verify** — state what you expect to find, then pull the data that could prove you wrong. One disconfirmed hypothesis is worth more than three confirmed ones.
3. **Quantify or drop** — a finding without a number is an opinion. "Many abandoned carts" → "63 abandoned carts worth 9,400 SAR in 7 days, 41% containing product X".
4. **Cross-check with the team** — read the `council_board`; if a colleague's finding could explain yours (sales drop ↔ courier delays), consult them before recommending.
5. **Size the prize** — estimate the impact in SAR/month where possible, even roughly. The GM ranks by impact-vs-effort; help them.

## The recommendation quality bar
Reject your own recommendation if any of these fail:
- The owner could ask "why now?" and you have no data answer.
- The "How" steps don't name the exact Salla dashboard screens.
- It repeats something the owner already dismissed (check your memory).
- It needs a budget/tool the owner doesn't have (check the store profile).

## Confidence discipline
Label findings: **confirmed** (data shown), **probable** (one signal, needs a week of watching), **hypothesis** (worth checking, not worth acting on yet). Only confirmed findings become top-3 recommendations.

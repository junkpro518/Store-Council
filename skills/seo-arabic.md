# Arabic E-commerce SEO Playbook

## Keyword reality in KSA
- Buyers search in Arabic, dialect Arabic, AND transliterated English ("ايفون", "iPhone", "ابل"). Cover all three for top products.
- Commercial modifiers that convert: "اونلاين", "توصيل", "اصلي", "سعر", "الرياض/جدة" + city names.
- Long-tail Arabic questions ("ما الفرق بين…") are low-competition blog targets that feed product pages.

## Metadata patterns
- Title template: `اسم المنتج | الفائدة الأساسية | اسم المتجر` ≤ 60 chars.
- Meta description: lead with the offer (شحن مجاني، دفع عند الاستلام) — these win clicks in KSA SERPs.
- Every category page needs 100–200 words of unique Arabic intro text; empty category pages are the most common Salla SEO gap.

## Product content that ranks
- ≥150 words of unique Arabic description; manufacturer copy duplicated across stores ranks for nobody.
- Name images descriptively before upload; alt text in Arabic.
- Answer the 3 pre-sale questions buyers actually ask (mine the store's Q&A and reviews for them).

## Quick audit order
1. Store-level SEO settings (title/description) → 2. top-20 products by sales → 3. category pages → 4. content pages coverage.

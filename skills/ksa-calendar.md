# KSA/GCC Commerce Calendar

| Season | Timing | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Ramadan** | Hijri — shifts ~11 days earlier yearly | Biggest behavioral shift of the year: night-time shopping, gifts, dates/food, abaya/fashion. Prepare campaigns 3–4 weeks ahead; delivery promises must account for courier overload. |
| **Eid al-Fitr** | End of Ramadan | Gift peak: perfumes, fashion, kids. Last-ship dates are the critical message. |
| **Hajj / Eid al-Adha** | Hijri | Travel goods, gifts; shorter peak than Fitr. |
| **Back to school** | Aug–early Sep | Stationery, kids' fashion, electronics. |
| **Saudi National Day** | Sep 23 | Green-themed promos; storewide offers are customary (91, 92… % styled discounts). |
| **Founding Day** | Feb 22 | Growing promo occasion. |
| **White Friday** | Last Friday of Nov (+ week) | The discount event of the year; plan stock and margins 6 weeks ahead. |
| **Summer** | Jun–Aug | Travel + AC/cooling categories up; general retail dips — good window for catalog/SEO housekeeping. |

## How to use this
- Always check "what's coming in the next 4–6 weeks" and prep the owner **ahead** of the peak, not during.
- Inventory: stock decisions for White Friday and Ramadan happen 4–6 weeks early — coordinate with the Inventory Manager.
- Hijri dates move against the Gregorian calendar; verify the current year's dates rather than assuming last year's.

# GEO Playbook — Be the AI's Answer

## How generative engines pick what to cite
AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, AI Overviews) compose answers from content that is: **extractable** (clear facts, tables, Q&A), **unambiguous** (one entity, one name, explicit units), **corroborated** (consistent across pages/sources), and **directly question-shaped** (matches how people phrase asks). Ranking #4 on Google means little if the AI answer cites someone else.

## The GEO audit order
1. **Ask the engines** — phrase 5–10 real buyer questions for the store's niche («أفضل عطر رجالي بأقل من 300 ريال؟», "best X for Y in Saudi Arabia") and note who gets cited. The gap between those answers and this store IS the work queue.
2. **Entity clarity pass** — top-20 products: exact name (Arabic + English), brand spelled one way everywhere, specs as labeled facts with units, model numbers explicit. Ambiguity = uncitable.
3. **Question-shaped coverage** — every top product/category needs the 3–5 questions buyers actually ask answered *in plain prose* on-page (mine the store's own Q&A and reviews for phrasing).
4. **Comparison content** — "X vs Y" and "best X for [use case]" pages are what engines quote for purchase advice. Honest comparisons that include competitors get cited more, not less.
5. **Fact consistency** — shipping promise, return window, COD availability identical on every page; contradictions make engines drop the store as a source.

## Formats engines quote
- FAQ blocks (question as heading, 2–4 sentence direct answer first).
- Spec tables with units.
- Short definitive sentences near the top ("يصل خلال ٢٤ ساعة داخل الرياض").
- Substantive reviews (coordinate with the Reputation Manager — review *content* is GEO raw material).

## Measuring GEO (no rank trackers exist for AI answers)
- Re-run the question battery monthly; track citations/mentions of the store in a simple before/after table (save it to memory).
- Watch direct/branded traffic and "heard about you from ChatGPT" signals in customer questions.
- Treat each content fix as an experiment: question → current AI answer → change shipped → answer after 4–6 weeks.

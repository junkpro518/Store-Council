# Conversion & Checkout Playbook (KSA)

## Abandoned cart triage
Segment abandoned carts before recommending anything:
- **High value + known customer** → personal WhatsApp/email recovery beats generic discounts.
- **Shipping shock** pattern (abandonment clustered at checkout) → test threshold/flat-rate changes with Logistics.
- **Payment friction** (abandonment at payment step) → check method coverage below.
- Recovery timing: first nudge ≤ 3h, second at 24h with small incentive; more than two = spam.

## Payment mix expectations (KSA)
A store missing any of these is leaving conversions on the table: **Mada** (non-negotiable), **Apple Pay** (mobile-heavy traffic), **BNPL — Tabby/Tamara** (raises AOV 20–40% on fashion/electronics), **COD** (still demanded; pair with COD fee to offset return risk).

## Trust signals that move KSA conversion
- Visible reviews on product pages (coordinate with Reputation Manager).
- Clear return policy in Arabic, shipping time per city, official licenses (معروف/CR number) in the footer.
- Real photos > stock renders; size/spec tables for fashion/electronics.

## Measuring without analytics access
Use proxies: orders ÷ abandoned carts as a funnel-health ratio; track it weekly in metrics_history and flag >15% deterioration.
